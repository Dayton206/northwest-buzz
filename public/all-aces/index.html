01

Meta Ad Library · Facebook page · site homepage

Three of your own channels name three different service areas

This is the biggest thing in the audit and it has nothing to do with SEO. Your paid ads talk to Seattle. Your Facebook page says Seattle. Your website map says the counties north of Seattle. Your phone number is a 206.

One of those is right and the others are costing you money. I do not know which, and that is the first question I want to ask you, because everything downstream depends on the answer.

If you really work Snohomish, Skagit and Island, then every Seattle impression you have paid for since 17 May was aimed at somebody you cannot serve. If you really work Seattle, your website is turning away the exact buyers your ads just paid to warm up.

What it costs Wasted spend on out of area leads, plus a relevance problem. Google decides which towns to show you in partly by which towns you talk about. Right now you tell it two different stories.

03

Meta Ad Library · destination shown as FB.ME on every ad

Every ad sends the lead to Facebook, not to anything you own

All fourteen show FB.ME as the destination, which means instant forms inside Facebook. Instant forms are cheap per lead and they are genuinely fine for volume. But three things happen when nothing lands on your site.

You build no on-site audience to retarget later, so every campaign starts cold again. Your website gets none of the engagement signal that helps it rank. And you never learn what your own pages do to a buyer, because no buyer ever sees one.

You have built beautiful proof assets, the hammer test, the stretch test, the stain test, real before and afters, and the people you pay to reach never touch any of it.

What it costs Every dollar of Meta spend evaporates the day you pause it. Nothing accumulates.
04

Google Ads Transparency Center · verified advertiser

Two Google ads against fourteen on Meta

You are running one search ad, the Penntek four times stronger than epoxy one with sitelinks, and one display unit. That is it.

Meta is interruption. You are catching somebody mid-scroll who was not thinking about their garage floor. That works, and your creative is good at it. Google search is the opposite. Somebody typing garage floor coating near me has already decided. They are comparing installers, not discovering the category.

Right now you are heavily invested in the part of the funnel where you have to create the want, and barely present in the part where the want already exists.

Measurement pending

Monthly search volume and cost per click for coating terms across your actual service towns. I am pulling this before our call rather than guessing at it, because in a niche this specific the real numbers are often nothing like the assumption.

What it costs The highest intent buyer in the category is reaching somebody else's landing page.

06

Instagram + Facebook · 65 posts · 193 + 173 followers

Sixty-five posts averaging around 558 views

I counted the view number on eighteen consecutive reels. They ran from 320 to 1,039. Total across those eighteen: 10,038. That is an average of roughly 558 views per reel.

Here is what I want you to hear in that. The content is not the problem. You are filming on site, showing the grinder, showing cracked slabs, showing finished floors, putting your own face in it. That is exactly the right raw material and most contractors will not do it.

The problem is that 65 posts have produced 193 followers and around 558 views a piece, which means the work is being made and then dropped into a channel with no distribution system under it. Same footage, structured and published differently, does not do 558.

What it costs You are already paying the hard cost, which is filming. You are just not collecting on it.

What all seven have in common

Nothing you are doing accumulates

Rented

Meta ads, instant forms, boosted posts. The day the card stops, the leads stop, and you are back to zero with nothing to show for the spend except a list of names.

Owned

Pages on your domain about towns you serve. A profile that ranks in those towns. A library of your own footage indexed and searchable. These keep working on a month you spend nothing.

You have nine years of work, real proof footage, certified installs and a genuine point of view about prep. Almost none of it is parked anywhere permanent. That is the whole opportunity.
